# BlueWallet-lif Tasks
# LIF coin wallets in BlueWallet (lifcoin/, on the anycoin layer)

## Open Tasks

- Add Wallet: the texts of the Lifcoin option ("Lifcoin", "LIF wallet, on
  the LIF network") are not translated: English in every language.
- LIF wallet kinds still missing (Bitcoin has them). Each needs its base
  class to use this.network / this.electrum (anycoin), a LIF variant
  (lif_wallet_class) and its way in:
  - Watch-only: a bare Lpub that was never used is imported as SegWit
    (p2wpkh), also when it is the key of a Legacy or Taproot wallet: its
    addresses are then wrong. A descriptor or [fp/path]Lpub says the kind.
    To do: offer the four kinds in the Discovery list (needs a name per
    kind). ypub / zpub texts are checked on Bitcoin only.
  - Watch-only with an external signer: the screen that shows the PSBT was
    run, and signing + combining in the page. Giving the signed PSBT back
    through the screen (scan, file) was not run. Taproot: cosignPsbt does
    not finalize (same on Bitcoin).
  - Import of a private key with a script prefix (p2wpkh:WIF): Bitcoin
    only.
  - Multisig vault, not done: a signature given from outside (scan, file)
    was not run; other formats than 2 of 3 P2WSH were not run; a
    cosigner key given as an xpub with a LIF path is not converted to
    Ypub/Zpub (class/multisig-cosigner.ts does it for the Bitcoin paths);
    texts of the "Lifcoin Vault" option are not translated.
  - aezeed (the seed of the lnd Lightning node): no LIF wallet for it. Its
    path is fixed to Bitcoin's in class/wallets/hd-aezeed-wallet.ts.
- The LIF kinds other than HDlif (HD and single-address: Legacy, P2SH,
  Taproot, single-address SegWit): a real send on the LIF network was not
  done yet (signing was tested with a fake coin).
- Add Wallet, kind menu (more_horiz) with Lifcoin selected: "Lightning" is

- Wallet details of a LIF wallet: the button says "Show Wallet XPUB" (the
